From open-source experiment to mature tool: Sossoldi officially reaches Version 1.0!

What v1.0 brings:
- Security & Architecture: Safe editing of categories/accounts without losing past budget history.
- Speed: Faster daily tracking with quick shortcuts and updated settings.
- A LOT of bug fixed
- iOS Return: Catching up with subcategories, drag-and-drop, and Face ID fixes.

A huge thank you to all the community who contributed code, reports and ideas to make this achievement possible!
